The NeCS PhD Winter School 2027 will take place at [Hotel Bellavista](https://fiemme.hotelbellavista.eu/) from 1 to 5 February 2027 in Cavalese, Trento, Italy.

**Half-board accommodation is included in the registration fee.**

Cavalese is a charming alpine town in Trentino and the historic centre of Val di Fiemme. Surrounded by the Dolomites and the Lagorai mountain range, it combines spectacular mountain scenery, a lively historic centre, local traditions and excellent cuisine. In winter, the area is renowned for skiing, cross-country skiing and snow activities.

## How to get there

If you reach Val di Fiemme by train, the closest station is Ora, and from there you can take a 45-minute bus ride to Cavalese. 
If you reach by plane there is a FlySki shuttle service from several nearby airports.  
For further options and more detailed information, please see [Visit Fiemme’s travel guide](https://www.visitfiemme.it/en/area/mobility/how-to-get-there) for travel options by plane, car, bus and train.
